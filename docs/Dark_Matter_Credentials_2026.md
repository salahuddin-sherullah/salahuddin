## 01 / An introduction

### When better decisions depend on better evidence

Dark Matter helps organizations clarify difficult questions, make sense of complex information and turn evidence into practical choices. Our work brings together research and strategy, data analysis and storytelling, and the thoughtful adoption of AI.

We focus on the intersection of development, public policy and human outcomes. We help teams define the problem before committing to a solution, understand what the available evidence can support, and communicate findings in ways that people can use.

Our services support nonprofits refining programs, foundations assessing priorities, research teams planning studies, and organizations seeking stronger analysis or more effective ways of working.

The approach is straightforward: start with the decision, investigate carefully, and produce something useful. That might be a strategy memo, a monitoring framework, an interactive dashboard, a public-facing explainer or an AI adoption roadmap.

## 02 / What we do

### Our services

### Research & Strategy

We help organizations define the decisions they need to make, identify gaps in their understanding and translate research into strategic direction.

- **Research scoping:** Frame research questions, audiences and intended uses; develop focused studies and learning agendas.
- **Strategic diagnosis:** Examine context, constraints and assumptions to identify the underlying problem and relevant tradeoffs.
- **Policy and program input:** Translate findings into practical recommendations for leaders, program teams and partners.
- **Monitoring and evaluation:** Develop results chains, monitoring and evaluation frameworks, and indicators that keep programs focused on meaningful outcomes.

**Typical outputs:** Strategy memos, research briefs, issue maps, interview syntheses, learning agendas, monitoring and evaluation frameworks, and indicator matrices. [Service details][S2]

### Data & Storytelling

We help teams turn fragmented or underused data into analysis, visuals and narratives that make the implications easier to understand.

- **Data preparation and analysis:** Clean, combine and interrogate datasets around clearly defined questions.
- **Visualization and dashboards:** Build charts, summaries and custom interactive dashboards that reveal patterns and support exploration.
- **Complex and geospatial data:** Analyze location, distribution and operational context using large or spatial datasets.
- **Insight communication:** Shape findings into briefs, presentations, report graphics and narratives suited to their audience.

**Typical outputs:** Analytical memos, data briefs, custom dashboards, geospatial visuals, slide decks, report graphics and executive summaries. [Service details][S3]

### AI Adoption

We help organizations move from isolated experiments to useful, repeatable ways of working with AI.

- **Use-case discovery:** Identify practical opportunities across research, writing, documentation, analysis and internal operations.
- **Workflow improvement:** Redesign recurring tasks to support faster drafting, synthesis and triage.
- **Adoption guidance:** Support tool selection, prompting practices, quality checks and clear expectations about limitations.
- **Training and capacity building:** Deliver hands-on learning around the tasks staff actually perform.

**Typical outputs:** Use-case maps, workflow redesign notes, tool recommendations, adoption roadmaps, training sessions, prompting guidance and pilot summaries. [Service details][S4]

## 06 / Our strengths

### Making difficult data accessible

Useful evidence is often scattered across reports, administrative records and inconsistent formats. Our work brings those inputs together, makes limitations visible and gives readers a clearer way to explore the subject.

**In practice:** *Gilgit-Baltistan at a glance* brings regional development information into a visual narrative, explaining the difficulties of finding and extracting data, including from scanned documents. [View the project][S7]

### How we work

1. **Frame the need.** Define the decision, intended audience, core questions and useful outputs.
2. **Examine the evidence.** Review sources, data quality, gaps, assumptions and the need for further inquiry.
3. **Develop and refine.** Build the analysis, narrative, framework or workflow through focused iteration.
4. **Make it usable.** Translate the work into clear outputs and, where relevant, guidance that helps the team sustain the approach.

This sequence brings together the methods described across the [research][S2], [data][S3] and [AI adoption][S4] services.

## 07 / Selected work

### Evidence, explained

These examples are published in the project website under Salahuddin's name. They illustrate research and communication capabilities; they do not imply separate Dark Matter client commissions.

### Pakistan's power landscape

**The question:** How do the technical, financial and political parts of Pakistan's electricity system fit together?

**The work:** A nine-part series covering the electricity ecosystem, prices, capacity payments, losses, debt, politics, residential and industrial demand, and solar adoption.

**What it demonstrates:** Structuring a complex policy subject into a connected narrative that readers can explore one issue at a time.

[Explore the series][S6]

### Gilgit-Baltistan at a glance

**The question:** What can available data tell us about a region often understood primarily as a tourist destination?

**The work:** A regional data narrative examining topics including population, the economy, tourism, agriculture and energy, while discussing source availability and reliability.

**What it demonstrates:** Evidence gathering in a data-constrained setting, analytical synthesis and accessible visualization.

[View the regional analysis][S7]

### The Gilgit-Baltistan budget

**The question:** Where does the region's public money come from, and what does it pay for?

**The work:** An interactive explanation using a 100-rupee framework to make revenue sources and spending choices easier to follow, alongside analysis of development allocations and geographic distribution.

**What it demonstrates:** Translating public finance into an understandable visual story while distinguishing illustrative shares from year-specific figures.

[View the budget explainer][S8]
